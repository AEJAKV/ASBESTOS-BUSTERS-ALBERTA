FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Start with
a little clarity.

Straightforward answers to help you prepare for an assessment or plan your next project.

Can I identify asbestos just by looking at it?+

No. Appearance alone cannot confirm whether a material contains asbestos. Appropriate sampling and laboratory analysis are needed to identify it. Avoid disturbing suspect materials while you arrange advice.

Should I test before renovating an older property?+

Before cutting, sanding or removing suspect building materials, arrange an assessment. Alberta Health Services recommends testing materials that may contain asbestos before renovations in buildings built before the early 1990s.

Does finding asbestos mean everything must be removed?+

Not necessarily. The material’s condition, its location and whether planned work will disturb it all matter. An assessment can help determine the appropriate approach for the specific property.

What affects the cost of a project?+

The type and amount of material, access, containment needs, waste handling and project requirements all affect the scope. Share your project details so the next step can be discussed before a quotation is prepared.

Can people stay in the property during the work?+

That depends on the work area, the project plan and the controls required. Occupancy and access should be discussed before work is scheduled; do not assume a space can remain occupied.

How long will the work take?+

Timing depends on the assessment, laboratory turnaround where needed, the work involved and project scheduling. A useful timeline starts with a defined scope rather than a fixed promise.

What should I include in my enquiry?+

Tell us the service you need, your Alberta region, the property type and a short description of the work. Mention any existing reports. Please do not disturb materials to gather more information.

Can I send a sample myself?+

Please do not cut, scrape or remove suspect material to send with an enquiry. Describe the concern in the contact form and arrange professional assessment before disturbing it.

Which Alberta areas can I enquire about?+

You can submit projects in the Calgary region, Edmonton region, Central Alberta, Southern Alberta and Northern Alberta. Availability and travel requirements are confirmed when your project is reviewed.
