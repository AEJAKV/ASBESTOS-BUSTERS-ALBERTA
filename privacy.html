This notice explains the information requested by this website and how it is used.

What the form collects

The contact form requests your name, email, Alberta region, property type, service of interest and project message. It also asks for your consent to receive a response about the enquiry.

Why it is collected

The details you submit are used to understand your request, reply by email and discuss appropriate next steps. The form does not subscribe you to a marketing list.

Form processing

When enabled, this website uses Web3Forms to transmit and process form submissions. The provider may process data outside Canada. You can review Web3Forms’ privacy policy for information about its practices. The website does not save your form entries in your browser.

Website technology

This website does not include advertising trackers or analytics scripts. The hosting and form providers may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and protect their services.

Your choices

Only include the details needed to explain your project. Do not submit sensitive personal or financial information. To ask about information you have submitted, or request a correction or deletion, use the contact form and describe your request.
